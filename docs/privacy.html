Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

Click Trace does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data.

What the extension accesses

  • It runs on a page only after you open it from the toolbar or with a keyboard shortcut (the activeTab permission).
  • It reads element positions and computed styles on that page to display measurements. This information never leaves your browser.
  • It makes no network requests.

What is stored

  • Your preferences (theme, accent color, units, grid settings, toolbar position and recently picked colors) are saved with chrome.storage.local on your device.
  • Screenshots are saved to your Downloads folder and copied to your clipboard. They are never uploaded.
  • Design mockups you add to the overlay stay in memory on that page and are discarded when you close the tab.
